### Who we are 
Rewire Consultancy is a Calgary based psychological consulting firm aimed at leveraging the principles of behavioural psychology to design and implement evidence-based solutions. 

 ### Our Mission
 *To transform organizations and communities by applying the science of behaviour to make better and more efficient systems.*

 ### Our Vision
 *To be the leading consultancy in the application of behavioural science, recognized for innovative solutions and measurable impact.*
 

### What we do
Seeing the struggle of many organisations to achieve optimal performance and the inability of traditional intervention strategies to consider the related psychological factors affecting decision-making, motivation and collaboration, we deduced a unique approach of integrating behavioural science into system development.

Offering customised and tailored plans for our clients, we help them to understand and influence human behaviour in order to achieve their individual goals. These can include system effectiveness, employee well-being, customer satisfaction or student outcomes.
