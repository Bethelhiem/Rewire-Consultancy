We use multiple assessment strategies and tools such as the following to formulate strategic interventions.
- *Surveys and questionnaires,*
- *Interviews with employees and managers,*
- *Workplace observations,*
- *Data analysis using statistical software such as SPSS and R,*
- *Regular progress monitoring and evaluation.*

Deliverables of our services:
- *Comprehensive behavioural audit report.*
- *Detailed intervention plan.*
- *Training materials for managers and employees.*
- *Regular progress reports and data analysis.*
- *Final evaluation report summarizing the impact of the interventions.*
